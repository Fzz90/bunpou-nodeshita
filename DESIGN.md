# Bunpou Jawir: ～のでした (Tema Manga & Anime Tanpa Karakter)

## Brief
Seven presentation slides in Indonesian for **Faiz Syihab, NIM 125241046, Kelas A** (`Bunpou Jawir`). Teach **～のでした**, compare it with similar patterns (**～のです / ～んです**, **～からだった**, **～わけだった**), and demonstrate how to distinguish them interactively (no practice quizzes). Visual direction re-themed to **Japanese Manga Manuscript & Anime Production Board (*Koma-wari*, *Genga*, & *Fumikiri* scenery)** with **strictly zero characters or human figures**.

## Design Read & Dials
Reading this as: **interactive Japanese grammar manga-editorial slide deck** for **university learners**, in a **printed manga manuscript & anime storyboard visual language**, dial **ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2**.
